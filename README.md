# Packrat for Android

Packrat creates, inspects and extracts ZIP archives. By Rednave.

## Install

Requires Android 10 or newer (best on Android 13+).

1. Copy the APK file to your phone (USB, Bluetooth, cloud drive — whatever you like) and tap it.
2. Allow "install from unknown sources" when asked.
3. Open the app from your app drawer.

## Using Packrat

- Open a ZIP to see a verified contents list with counts and total size. Filter or sort the entries.
- Select files to pack them into a new ZIP, which is verified after writing.
- Extract into a new folder; existing files are never overwritten.

ZIP only: no encrypted archives and no TAR, 7z or RAR. Large archives are limited to 2,000 entries and 256 MB total.

## Look and feel

Open About inside the app to choose between Aqua, Bliss, Dark Bliss and Super, and to adjust motion. Aqua is the default.
